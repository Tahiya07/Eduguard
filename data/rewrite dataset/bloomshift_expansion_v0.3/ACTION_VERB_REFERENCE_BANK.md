# BloomShift action-verb reference bank

Purpose: diversify the *opening action verbs* in draft question rewrites while aligning the required cognitive operation with Revised Bloom's Taxonomy. This is a practical, curated bank, not a claim that any verb uniquely determines a level. A verb may appear at multiple levels; the actual task, evidence, and reasoning demand decide alignment.

## Remember
Identify, Define, List, State, Recall, Name, Label, Match, Select, Locate, Recognize, Enumerate, Quote, Reproduce, Sequence, Count, Tabulate, Retrieve.

## Understand
Explain, Summarize, Interpret, Classify, Paraphrase, Illustrate, Describe, Distinguish, Compare, Infer, Translate, Exemplify, Outline, Restate, Categorize, Predict, Clarify, Relate.

## Apply
Apply, Calculate, Demonstrate, Implement, Use, Solve, Execute, Compute, Operate, Modify, Illustrate, Prepare, Construct, Complete, Determine, Perform, Adapt, Show.

## Analyze
Analyze, Differentiate, Examine, Decompose, Trace, Investigate, Categorize, Contrast, Dissect, Separate, Attribute, Organize, Map, Relate, Diagnose, Inspect, Compare, Distinguish.

## Evaluate
Evaluate, Justify, Critique, Assess, Defend, Validate, Appraise, Rank, Recommend, Judge, Verify, Prioritize, Argue, Rate, Support, Select, Challenge, Weigh.

## Create
Design, Construct, Develop, Formulate, Propose, Compose, Devise, Generate, Invent, Plan, Synthesize, Engineer, Produce, Assemble, Draft, Build, Create, Model.

## Usage constraints
- Choose an opening verb that naturally describes the *required student operation*, not merely a verb associated with the target level.
- Do not mechanically replace a WH-word with a verb while leaving the cognitive operation unchanged.
- Preserve named entities, technical terms, quantities, conditions, constraints, negation, requested number of items, and supplied context.
- Do not invent missing context or facts. If a transformation cannot be made answerable and meaning-preserving, mark it not feasible rather than fabricating context.
- Keep the question form appropriate to the task: an instruction may start with an imperative verb and still end with a question mark if it is phrased as a question, but do not force punctuation unnaturally.
- These lists were cross-checked against university teaching/assessment resources; they are not exhaustive and verb-to-level mapping is not deterministic.
